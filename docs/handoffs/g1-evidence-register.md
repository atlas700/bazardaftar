R010 — correction questions: a weight/quantity writing mistake was crossed out and
the correct value written beside it. The founder confirmed this fixed a writing

owner or manager was allowed to approve; the particular approver remains
unattributed. This is reported practice for a writing correction, not permission
for managers to adjust posted stock or debt under the application's owner-only
correction policy.

R011 — owner-review question: the founder saw the owner review stock totals and
supplier debts/payments. Exact reporting format, totals, and physical-count
reconciliation were not supplied.

R012 — purchase/acceptance questions: the founder watched price and quantity being
agreed and the delivery for the **same purchase**. Owner and manager agreed
together; after clarification, only the owner could decide alone. Delivered weight
matched the agreed quantity, and the manager accepted the delivery after weighing.
No receipt variance or variance approval was observed in this example.

R013 — participant questions: owner P001 and manager P004 each identified stock
accuracy and supplier balances/payments as difficulties. The founder could recall

R014 — purchase-example questions: the delivery contained both grains, recorded

are not current market prices, product defaults, exact financial reconciliation,

- Use business codes B001 onward, participant codes P001 onward, and separate
  interview/visit references. Link repeat visits to the same business and repeat
  interviews to the same participant; do not inflate totals with follow-ups.
- Record the reconstruction date separately from the original event date.
  Preserve approximate dates and unknowns. Label later recollections explicitly.
- For each workflow, record what the founder recalls directly observing, what
  staff explained, or what remains unknown. Attach a record reference to each
  finding; do not copy the common-workflow summary into individual businesses.
  The 1 November acceptance changes the gate decision, not these evidence rules.
- Keep identities, contacts, raw notes, transcripts, and financial records outside
  the repository. An anonymous reference must identify a retrievable source;
  do not claim a private note exists until one has actually been created.
- Use the existing [fieldwork checklist](pilot-research-fieldwork.md) for details
  and follow-up visits. A partial record remains partial until its gaps are reviewed.
